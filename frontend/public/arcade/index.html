How the arcade teaches

Five rules, borrowed from how memory actually works.

1

See it first. Ten-frames, arrays, plates and number lines show the structure before the symbol.

2

Recall, don't reread. Every question asks you to produce the answer, not recognise it.

3

Miss it, see it. A wrong answer holds and opens the worked method for that exact fact.

4

Fifty per deck. A full pass over a topic, drawn from facts you have not seen recently.

5

Misses come back. Facts you get wrong return at the front of the next deck until they stick.
